## What it is

The analytics platform of bekumoo Software GmbH, the product I work on as a Fullstack Developer.
I joined the company as a dual-study student in 2023 and started on Power BI and Azure Synapse
reporting. Over time the work moved up the stack into data engineering, and since July 2026 I
work as a full-time developer on both sides of the platform: a Python/FastAPI backend and a
React/TypeScript frontend.

## What I do

- **Frontend.** Most of the React and TypeScript frontend of the customer app for car
  dealerships, with the focus on getting numbers on screen in a way people can trust and read.
- **Backend.** Python and FastAPI endpoints that serve data to the application.
- **Security.** A security review of the customer applications with Semgrep, gitleaks,
  osv-scanner and Checkov, plus login hardening and row-level security.
